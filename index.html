Our Story

Founded in 2015, Coffee Bliss began as a small corner roastery with a big dream: to bring the world's finest beans to your neighborhood.

Every morning, our master roasters hand-select single-origin beans from sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. We roast in small batches to preserve the unique character of each origin.

Come for the coffee, stay for the community. Our shop is more than a café — it's a gathering place for creators, dreamers, and coffee lovers.
