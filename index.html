Our Story

Founded in 2015, Coffee Bliss began with a simple dream: to bring the world's finest beans to your cup. Every morning, our master roasters carefully select single-origin beans from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

We believe coffee is more than a drink — it's a ritual, a connection, and a moment of joy. From our cozy corner café to your morning commute, we're here to make every sip unforgettable.
